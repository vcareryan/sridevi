SECTION 3 - HOW IT WORKS (4 Phases)
=====================================

Small Tag:
How It Works

Heading:
From Script to Screen in 4 Phases

Phase 1 Title:
Script Development

Phase 1 Description:
Every great film begins with a powerful story. Our creative team works closely with writers to develop screenplays that captivate, challenge, and inspire audiences across the globe.

Phase 2 Title:
Pre-Production

Phase 2 Description:
Meticulous planning including casting, location scouting, storyboarding, budgeting, and assembling our world-class crew to ensure every detail is perfect before cameras roll.

Phase 3 Title:
Production & Filming

Phase 3 Description:
State-of-the-art cinematography, expert direction, and seamless coordination bring the script to life. We use cutting-edge technology and creative excellence on every set.

Phase 4 Title:
Post-Production & Release

Phase 4 Description:
Professional editing, color grading, VFX, sound design, and music composition followed by strategic distribution across theatrical, OTT, and international markets.
