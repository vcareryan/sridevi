## SECTION 8: FAQ

**Heading:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What genres does Sridevi Bunglow Films produce?**
A1: We specialize in psychological thrillers, drama, and suspense. However, we are open to compelling stories across all genres that have the potential to create impact and resonate with audiences worldwide.

**Q2: How can I submit a script or story idea?**
A2: You can submit your screenplay through our contact form. Our creative team reviews all submissions and responds within 30 days. We look for original, powerful stories with strong characters and unique perspectives.

**Q3: Does Sridevi Bunglow Films work with new talent?**
A3: Absolutely. We believe in discovering fresh talent — actors, directors, writers, and technicians. If you have exceptional skill and passion for cinema, we'd love to hear from you.

**Q4: In which languages do you produce films?**
A4: We produce films primarily in Hindi, with plans to expand into Malayalam, Tamil, and Telugu. Our vision is to create pan-Indian cinema that transcends language barriers.

**Q5: How can I invest in or co-produce a film?**
A5: We welcome investment and co-production partnerships. Please reach out through our contact form with details about your interest, and our team will schedule a confidential discussion about current opportunities.

**Q6: Where are Sridevi Bunglow Films movies distributed?**
A6: Our films are distributed across theatrical releases, major OTT platforms, and international markets. We work with top distribution partners to ensure maximum reach and audience engagement.
